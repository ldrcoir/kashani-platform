A136030 Range Statistics
================================================================================

For each range [lo, hi], shows:
  - Total primes: count of prime numbers in range
  - Mersenne: count of Mersenne prime exponents in range
  - Need factor: total primes minus Mersenne exponents
  - Found: count of primes where we have a smallest factor
  - Missing: count of primes still marked -1
  - Coverage: Found / Need factor * 100
